A house stands on a parcel. The editor can pick a cadastral parcel when a project is created, or attach one in the **Site** panel.

## add-site

```bash
add-site --json '{…}'
```

Attaches one normalized cadastral parcel snapshot.

## update-site

```bash
update-site --x 4000 --y 3000 --rotation 12
update-site --setback 2m
update-site --edge 2 --edge-setback 3.5m
update-site --surface s1 --material concrete-pavers
```

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `--x`, `--y`, `--rotation` | Place the house on its parcel |
| `--setback` | Setback from every boundary |
| `--edge`, `--edge-setback` | Setback from one boundary |
| `--site` | JSON ground cutout, surfaces, markings and railings |
| `--surface` with `--material` or `--colour` | Change one surface |

Geometry edits respect the parcel and its setbacks.

## remove-site

Detaches the parcel without changing the house.

## Outdoor rooms

Terraces, paving, paths and lawns are rooms with `--kind terrace|paving|path|lawn`. They have open edges and no ceiling. A `winter-garden` has glazed walls and a glazed roof.
