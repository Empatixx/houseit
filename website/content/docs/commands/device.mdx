---
title: Device
description: add-device, update-device, remove-device
---

Electrical devices hang on one face of an independent wall. The device stores the wall, a distance along it, a height and a side, and follows the wall when it moves or is split at a junction.

```bash
add-device --kind socket --wall w3 --along 400 --height 300
update-device --id d1 --along 900
remove-device --id d1
```

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `--kind` | Required. `socket`, `switch`, `light` or `panel` |
| `--wall` | Required. The wall |
| `--along` | Required. Distance from the wall's start |
| `--height` | Required. Height above the wall's base |
| `--side` | `a` or `b`, the face of the wall |

A panel with circuits stays until its circuits are removed. Full electrical, plumbing and HVAC workflows are not there yet; the hosts are groundwork for them.
